# Round response format

Use this fixed format for every substantive question round in Plan mode, including material-assisted discussion. The purpose is to make the conversation cumulative and teach the user how each answer changes the diligence plan.

Do not reproduce the entire final checklist in every round. Show the current top risks and the affected or provisionally scoped items in each of the three layers. Mark an unchanged section `本轮无实质变化` rather than omitting it. The complete three-layer checklist remains subject to the three internal reviews before final delivery.

## Contents

1. Required section order
2. Handling `请建议`
3. Round-to-round update rules

## Required section order

### 第 X 轮｜本轮讨论目标

State the current phase, what the round is trying to resolve, and whether the project picture is still preliminary.

### 1. 用户反馈及项目理解更新

| 用户反馈或新资料 | 事实状态 | 对项目理解的更新 | 相比上一轮的变化 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use `已确认`, `文件陈述`, `合理推断`, `Skill建议/暂定假设`, `存在冲突`, or `尚待确认`. Never present a Skill suggestion as a user-confirmed fact.

### 2. Teaching｜为什么本轮反馈会改变尽调

Explain the most important causal links in plain language:

`用户反馈 → 风险假设变化 → 需要什么证据 → 哪项尽调工作随之变化`

Identify at least one beginner misconception when useful. Do not merely say that the information is “important”.

### 3. 当前风险图（滚动更新）

| 优先级 | 风险主题 | 触发事实或假设 | 当前判断 | 本轮变化 | 下一步区分证据 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep the current P0/P1 risks visible. Use `新增`, `升高`, `降低`, `深化`, `暂缓`, or `无变化`. Explain every priority change.

### 4. **基础尽调事项**（本轮快照）

| 优先级 | 工作流及基础事项 | 当前状态 | 本轮调整 | Teaching：为什么调整 | 下一步 | 依据及对应尽调关注内容 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Show affected baseline matters and any matter needed to understand the current discussion. Use `拟新增`, `拟深化`, `拟调级`, `待判断`, `本轮无实质变化`, or `不适用待确认`. Label this a provisional snapshot, not the final complete baseline.

### 5. **业务及专项尽调细节**（本轮快照）

| 映射的基础事项 | 专项细节 | 当前状态 | 本轮调整 | 证据及程序方向 | Teaching：为什么 | 依据及对应尽调关注内容 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Map every detail back to Layer 1. Do not create an unrelated second checklist.

### 6. **行业、业务特点及近年监管关注**（本轮快照）

| 项目特征 | 关注方向 | 依据及对应尽调关注内容 | 本轮调整 | 对本项目的尽调启示 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish professional judgment, industry/business-model analogy, and verified regulatory case support. During early rounds, use `待检索核验` when appropriate. If a regulatory case is displayed, comply with all company-name, formal-title, and exact-page citation rules.

Across all round sections, never display a bare manual number or checklist ID as `依据`. Use:

`编号｜来源文件名称｜章节或事项标题｜本项对应的尽调关注内容`

Example:

`GEN-001 §6｜《通用尽调教学框架》｜客户、销售与收入｜关注内容：验证收入真实性、确认时点以及客户、交付和回款证据能否相互印证。`

### 7. 待确认事项及暂定假设

| 事项 | 当前处理 | 不确定性 | 若判断相反会怎样改变范围 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep unresolved material questions visible across rounds until resolved or explicitly retained as scope triggers.

### 8. 下一轮问题及Teaching

Ask one to three questions only.

| 编号 | 需要用户回复的问题 | 为什么需要回复 | 答案会改变什么 | 可参考的回答思路 | 没有思路时 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 一个宽泛、可理解的问题 | Explain the risk or scope decision | Identify affected priority, Layer 1, Layer 2, or Layer 3 | Give two or three non-leading dimensions, not a forced answer | 回复“第1题请建议” |

Every question must explain:

- why DD Mentor cannot safely treat the answer as irrelevant;
- which risk hypothesis, evidence chain, priority, or workstream it affects;
- what kind of answer is sufficient for a beginner.

### 9. 回复方式

End every question round with:

> 你可以按编号简要回答，不需要使用专业术语。对暂时没有思路的问题，可以回复“第2题请建议”；如果希望本轮全部由 DD Mentor 根据现有项目情况提出暂定建议，可以直接回复“请建议”。我会说明建议依据和不确定性，并继续推动下一轮。

## Handling “请建议”

If the user replies `请建议`, `第 N 题请建议`, or an equivalent expression:

1. answer the relevant question with a clearly labelled `Skill建议/暂定假设`;
2. state the confirmed project facts and sources supporting the suggestion;
3. state confidence as high, medium, or low and explain the principal uncertainty;
4. present a materially different alternative when one exists;
5. explain how the suggested assumption changes the risk map and each affected diligence layer;
6. continue to the next discussion phase without asking the user to invent an answer.

Do not fabricate a company fact. If a high-impact question cannot be reasonably inferred, recommend a conservative planning assumption, keep the fact in Open Items, show the alternative branch, and continue. Ask for confirmation only when the choice would materially change the transaction objective, legal authority, or an irreversible external action.

If the user provides some answers and asks for suggestions on others, treat the supplied answers as user feedback and the suggestions as separate provisional assumptions.

## Round-to-round update rules

After every user response:

1. compare the new facts with the prior round;
2. update fact status and resolve or preserve conflicts;
3. update the risk map;
4. update all three diligence layers in the same response;
5. explain each addition, removal, priority change, or deepening;
6. choose the next one to three questions from the remaining decisions with the greatest effect on scope.

Do not regenerate a generic plan from scratch. Preserve unchanged conclusions and show the delta. Do not claim the provisional snapshots are complete or reviewed until the required three internal reviews are finished.
